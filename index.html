About NCC at P.B. College

The National Cadet Corps (NCC) unit at Pramathesh Barua College plays a vital role in shaping the youth of the region. It instills qualities of leadership, discipline, and patriotism among students, preparing them to be responsible citizens and future leaders.

Our cadets actively participate in various training camps, social service activities, and adventure sports, contributing significantly to community development and national building.

Pramathesh Barua College,Gauripur

Pramathesh Barua College, located in Gauripur, Dhubri, Assam, is a renowned educational institution dedicated to providing quality education and fostering holistic development among its students. The college offers a wide range of academic programs and encourages participation in extracurricular activities like NCC.
