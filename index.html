About P.B. College NCC

Pramatesh Barua College, located in Gauripur, Dhubri, Assam, houses a disciplined NCC sub-unit under the 7 Assam BN NCC. Cadets undergo rigorous drill, weapon training, and community service.

National Cadet Corps

The NCC is the youth wing of the Indian Armed Forces. It opens doors for cadets to groom themselves into patriotic and responsible citizens.
