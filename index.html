About Our Unit

National Cadet Corps (NCC)

The National Cadet Corps is a premier youth organization in India, playing a pivotal role in molding the youth into disciplined, responsible, and patriotic citizens. Operating under the Ministry of Defence, it provides opportunities for the nation's youth for their overall development with a sense of duty, commitment, dedication, discipline, and moral values.

P.B. College NCC Unit

Affiliated under the 7 Assam BN NCC, Dhubri within the North Eastern Region (NER) Directorate, the P.B. College, Gauripur NCC unit stands as a beacon of excellence, shaping motivated youth through rigorous drill, weapon training, social outreach, and leadership camps.

Mission

To develop character, camaraderie, discipline, secular outlook, the spirit of adventure and ideals of selfless service amongst young citizens.

Vision

To create a human resource of organized, trained and motivated youth, to provide leadership in all walks of life and always be available for the service of the nation.

Rigorous Curriculum

Cadet Training Programs

Drill

Precision foot drill and ceremonial parades instilling split-second obedience, coordination, and posture.

Weapon Training

In-depth tactical instruction on handling, safety, and firing mechanics of standard military weaponry.

Map Reading

Mastering terrain analysis, grid references, compass navigation, and tactical field orientation.

Social Service

Dedicated community engagement projects emphasizing civic duty, health camps, and rural assistance.

Yoga

Routine physical conditioning, mental wellness, and stamina building through traditional yogic practice.

Adventure Activities

Trekking, obstacle courses, and survival expeditions designed to build courage and resilience.

Camps

CATC, ATC, and national integration camps promoting unified living and team leadership.

Community & Nation Building

Key Activities

Tree Plantation

Environmental drives across Gauripur to promote green cover and sustainable ecological practices.

Swachh Bharat

Active cleanliness campaigns within college grounds and surrounding municipal zones.

Blood Donation

Organizing voluntary blood donation camps in coordination with local medical authorities.

Independence Day Parade

Spectacular ceremonial guard of honor and national flag hoisting parades on 15th August.

Republic Day Parade

Displaying pristine military discipline and marching salutes during Republic Day celebrations.

Awareness Programmes

Rallies and seminars educating society on road safety, digital literacy, and health welfare.
