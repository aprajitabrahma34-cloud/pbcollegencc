About Our Unit

About P.B. College NCC

Pramatesh Barua College, located in Gauripur, Dhubri, Assam, houses a highly disciplined and active NCC sub-unit under the 7 Assam BN NCC. Our cadets undergo rigorous training blending academic pursuits with national service.

About National Cadet Corps

The NCC in India is a voluntary organization which recruits cadets from high schools, colleges and universities all over India. It is a prime youth movement infusing values of secularism, selfless service, and character.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To create a motivated, disciplined, and patriotic youth force.
Vision: Empowering young minds to emerge as future leaders of the Indian Armed Forces and civilian leadership.

Training Modules

Drill

Weapon Training

Map Reading

Social Service
